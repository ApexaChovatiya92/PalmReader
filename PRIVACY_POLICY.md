# Aura Palm – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 28, 2026_

Aura Palm is designed to work entirely offline. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary
- **We do not collect any data.** Aura Palm has no accounts, servers, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
- **Everything stays on your device.** Palm photos are analyzed on your iPhone or iPad and are never uploaded or shared.

## Camera
The camera is used only while you are scanning your palm, to find your hand and capture a photo for analysis. Camera images are processed on your device.

## Photos
If you choose a photo from your library, iOS gives Aura Palm access to that single photo only. It is analyzed on your device.

## Saved readings and notes
When you tap **Save Reading**, the reading, the palm photo, and any notes you write are stored locally in the app on your device. They are not sent anywhere. You can delete any saved reading in Reading History; deleting the app removes all of its data.

## Settings
Your language choice and whether you have seen the welcome notice are stored locally on your device.

## Sharing
If you use the Share button, the reading text is passed to the app or person you choose through the standard iOS share sheet. Aura Palm does not see or store what happens after that.

## Children
Aura Palm does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
